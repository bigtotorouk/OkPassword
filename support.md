# OKPassword Support

Need help with OKPassword? Contact us at **bigtotorouk@gmail.com**.

Please include your device model, iOS version, OKPassword version, and a brief description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful if sensitive information is hidden.

**Never send passwords, authenticator secrets, master passwords, backup passwords, or unencrypted vault exports.**

## Getting Started

OKPassword stores your passwords locally on your device. You do not need to create an account.

Add your login details manually or import a supported CSV file. Use search, favorites, and categories to organize your accounts.

## Unlocking Your Vault

Depending on your settings, you can unlock OKPassword using device authentication or a configured master password.

If device authentication fails:

1. Confirm that a device passcode is enabled.
2. Check that Face ID or Touch ID is available in your device settings.
3. Try again, or use your master password if you previously configured one.

We cannot recover forgotten master passwords or remotely unlock your vault.

## Setting Up AutoFill

Open the AutoFill guide in OKPassword and follow the instructions to enable OKPassword as a password provider in your device’s Settings.

The location of this setting varies by iOS version. Search Settings for **AutoFill** if needed.

When signing in to a compatible website or app, select OKPassword and authenticate to choose a saved login. If a matching account does not appear, open the credential picker and search manually.

## Backing Up and Restoring

Before changing devices or reinstalling OKPassword:

1. Open **Settings → Import, backup & restore**.
2. Create an encrypted backup.
3. Choose a separate backup password.
4. Save the file somewhere safe and keep its password securely.

To restore, choose your backup file, enter its password, review the contents, and select the available merge or replace option.

**We cannot recover a forgotten backup password.** Keep both the backup file and its password. Do not rely solely on a system backup.

## Importing and Exporting

OKPassword supports CSV import with field mapping and a preview before you confirm.

If an import fails, check that the file is a supported CSV file and that its columns are mapped correctly.

CSV exports are **not encrypted** and may include passwords and authenticator secrets. Use encrypted backups when possible, and remove unnecessary plaintext copies from storage and recently deleted folders.

## Authenticator Codes

You can add a supported authenticator account by scanning its setup QR code or entering the setup details manually.

If a code is rejected, check that your device’s date and time are correct and that the account’s authenticator settings match the original setup.

Keep recovery codes supplied by the website or service in a safe place. OKPassword support cannot reset another service’s two-factor authentication.

## Purchases and Restore

OKPassword Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple.

If you previously purchased Pro, use **Restore Purchases** from the purchase screen while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

If access is still unavailable, contact us with a description of the issue. Do not send payment card details or your Apple Account password.

For billing and refund requests, visit [Apple’s Report a Problem website](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Missing Data or Upgrade Issues

If your saved information does not appear after an update, avoid deleting or reinstalling the app before contacting support. Removing the app may remove local data needed for recovery.

Tell us which version you upgraded from, if known, and describe any error message. Do not send your password database unless a safe support process has been explicitly agreed upon.
