# Privacy Policy for OKPassword

**Effective Date: October 22, 2026**

OKPassword is developed by bing.xiao (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information Stored on Your Device

OKPassword is a local password manager. Information you enter or import may include:

- Account names, usernames, passwords, and website addresses.
- Notes, categories, tags, and favorites.
- Authenticator secrets used to generate time-based verification codes.
- Password generation history.
- Security settings, app preferences, and purchase entitlement information.

This information is processed and stored locally. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your vault, and you do not need to create an account with us.

## 2. How Your Information Is Used

OKPassword uses locally stored information to provide the features you choose, including password management, search, password generation, security checks, verification codes, AutoFill, and data import and export.

Password health checks run on your device. They do not send your passwords to an external breach-checking service and do not guarantee that an account is secure.

We do not sell your personal information, use your vault for advertising, or include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## 3. Local Storage and Security

The current vault format uses encryption. Encryption keys are protected using Apple Keychain, and device authentication is handled through Apple’s system security services.

OKPassword does not receive or store your Face ID or Touch ID biometric data.

If you upgrade from an older version, the app may retain the original database and a local recovery copy to help prevent migration-related data loss. These legacy files retain their original storage protections and are not automatically converted into the current encrypted vault format.

No storage or security method can guarantee absolute protection. Keep your device protected and store backup passwords securely. We cannot recover forgotten master passwords or backup passwords.

## 4. Password AutoFill

If you enable OKPassword in your device’s AutoFill settings, the app makes credential identifiers, such as website addresses and usernames, available to Apple’s local credential identity system.

The app and its AutoFill extension use an encrypted shared local container. When you select a credential, the requested login information is provided through Apple’s AutoFill system to the website or app you choose.

## 5. Camera Access

Camera access is optional and is used to scan authenticator QR codes. Scanning is processed on your device. OKPassword does not upload camera images or video to us.

You can manage camera permission in your device’s Settings.

## 6. Clipboard

When you choose to copy a password or verification code, OKPassword places it on the system clipboard. Copied secrets are marked for local-device use, and an expiration time is applied when clipboard expiration is enabled.

Another app may read copied information when permitted by the operating system. Clipboard expiration cannot remove information that another app has already read or saved.

## 7. Imports, Exports, and Backups

Files you select for import are processed locally.

Encrypted backups use a separate password that you choose. CSV exports are not encrypted and may contain passwords and authenticator secrets.

When you save or share an exported file, its destination may be another app, a cloud storage provider, or another location you select. That provider’s privacy policy applies to its handling of the file.

OKPassword does not provide its own cloud synchronization service. Device backups and files you save to cloud storage may be handled by Apple or another provider according to your settings.

## 8. Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. OKPassword uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to enable purchased features and restore purchases.

We do not receive your payment card details. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy.

## 9. Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve support issues.

Please do not send passwords, authenticator secrets, master passwords, backup passwords, or unencrypted vault exports.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to address your request and meet applicable obligations.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your vault remains on your device until you change or delete its contents or remove the app’s local data.

Deleting an entry from the current vault does not necessarily remove it from previously exported files, system backups, or retained legacy migration files. Copies outside the current vault must be managed separately.

Removing the app may remove its local files. Keychain items and copies held in backups or external storage may persist separately. Keep a verified encrypted backup before reinstalling the app or changing devices.

Because we do not hold your vault on our servers, we cannot access, retrieve, or remotely delete it.

For information you provide through support, you may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

OKPassword is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through support communications. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The effective date above will identify the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through the app or the page where this policy is published, as appropriate.
